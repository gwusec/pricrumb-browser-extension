About Functional Cookies:

Functional cookies are cookies used by websites to remember user's preferences (like language, region and font size), and are typically inserted only when a user performs an action (such as commenting on a blog or watching a video) but can also be used to enable features that have not been explicitly requested, and collects anonymized information on users without cross-site tracking.
